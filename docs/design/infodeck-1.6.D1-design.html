InfoDeck — v1.6 · D1 design

Two bug fixes. 1) About Me in 1.6 ignores the project standard almost entirely — this rebuilds it as ultra-compact-B, four rows, seven pieces of information, two-line footer, and the full mail pre-fill. 2) Sharing goes back to text only, exactly as in 1.5, and every file row gains its own Share button.

DESIGN ONLY · nothing built · no version bump Reply by tag: “S1.3 → smaller”, “Q2 → b”, or “defaults” = all recommended Bug 2 root cause named in S6 — I got the MIME type wrong
What 1.6 actually shipped for About Me, checked in the source: a plain card with the name, a dead line reading “Suggestion / Feedback / Bugs / Error →”, the e-mail as an underlined link with no subject or body pre-fill, LinkedIn and Facebook printed as full URLs on their own rows, no avatar, no “Developer” caption, no accent header strip, no feedback chips, no ✉ Send chip — and the whole card sits inside the collapsible “Diagnostics and About” group, when the standard says it must never be collapsible and must sit last, outside the accordion. Eight breaches. Every one is fixed below.
