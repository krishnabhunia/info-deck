C4 · QR codes

#PointDetail
1Generated on deviceA small encoder built into the app — roughly 100–150 KB, no service, no network Q11
2ContentPlain text — one value, or a labelled block for several. Any camera app reads it
3Error correctionLevel M, which survives a scratched print and stays readable at 1 024 px
4CapacityComfortable to about 1 200 characters. Beyond that, InfoDeck refuses to draw a dense unscannable code and offers one code per field instead
5On screenFull brightness while showing, restored on exit; the encoded text is always printed underneath
6Saving1 024 × 1 024 PNG with a white margin, through the system picker; or share the image directly
7Not a secretStated on screen. A QR is faster typing, not encryption

C6 · Privacy

Sharing is the one place data leaves InfoDeck, so the rules are explicit: nothing is shared without your tick; the exact text is previewed first; the app still holds no internet permission, so everything travels through an app you chose; QR codes are drawn locally; and the “not a secret” warning sits on the QR screen itself rather than in a help page.
