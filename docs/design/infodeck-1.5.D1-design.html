Three corrections: 1) the blue header block goes away entirely — a white 48 dp bar with a 3 dp gradient hairline, so the colour lives in the icons instead of a slab; 2) the bottom bar grows back from 50 dp to 58 dp with its words returned; 3) a proper colour system — nine hues, each with a fixed meaning, on tiles, chips, sections and tabs.

Why the blue slab has to go, not just shrink. A gradient block needs height to look deliberate — squeeze it under 48 dp and it reads as a mistake. Removing it entirely gives back 56 dp and solves your third point at the same time: with no blue field competing, coloured icons finally register.
