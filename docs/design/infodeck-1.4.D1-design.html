C2 · Android limits, stated plainly

These are platform rules, not choices. Every one of them is surfaced in the app rather than discovered when a reminder fails.
#LimitWhat InfoDeck does
1Full-screen intent (Android 14+)Needs a separate grant for apps that are not phone or clock apps. Without it, Alarm degrades to Ring — the Settings row says so, and the scheduler shows the fallback
2Exact alarmsAlready handled since 1.1; Alarm mode additionally uses setAlarmClock, which is the strongest guarantee Android offers
3Looping soundRing and Alarm need a short-lived foreground service to keep the sound alive, with its own quiet "InfoDeck is ringing" notification. That is the price of a sound that does not get cut off
4Aggressive battery managersSome phones kill background alarms. A row in Settings links straight to the manufacturer's battery screen
5New permissionsUSE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK. Still no INTERNET

C5 · What the extractor looks for

#KindRules
1Datesdd/mm/yyyy · dd-mm-yyyy · dd-MMM-yyyy · yyyy-mm-dd · “11 October 2026”. Day-first assumed (Indian documents), and any date that would be ambiguous is marked medium confidence
2Key dateWords near the date decide: expiry, valid upto, valid till, due, renewal, end date. A start/from date never wins the ★
3Labelled fields“<Label> : <value>” and “<Label> No. <value>” patterns — Policy No, Regn No, Chassis, Engine, IMEI, Serial, Invoice, Account, Customer ID, Premium, Amount
4Label for the cardThe strongest heading in the first page, trimmed to 60 characters
5Document typeinsurance · warranty · invoice · identity · other — used only to choose which reminders to propose
6Proposed remindersinsurance/warranty → Notify 30 days before + Alarm on the day · invoice → Notify 3 days before due · identity → Notify 6 months before Q7
7Confidencehigh = labelled and unambiguous · medium = pattern matched but the label was fuzzy · low = a bare value near a keyword. Only high is pre-ticked
8CeilingAt most 8 data, 4 dates and 3 reminders proposed, so the sheet stays readable
