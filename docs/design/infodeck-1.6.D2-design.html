InfoDeck — v1.6 · D2 design

You are right that 1.6's rows are big — I built the roomier reading of “bottom card deck” and it turned every card into a 76 dp block. This round makes the height yours to choose: three densities with plain names — Compact · Comfortable · Detailed — plus Tap to expand, which lets a dense list open one card in place without leaving the screen.

DESIGN ONLY · nothing built · no version bump Reply by tag: “S1 → Compact default”, “Q3 → b”, or “defaults” = all recommended
On the words. You suggested “expandable, compact, comfortable”. I kept Compact and Comfortable as you wrote them, named the fullest level Detailed (it says what you get, where “roomy” only says how it feels), and made Expandable a switch rather than a fourth density — because expanding is a behaviour you might want at any density, not a size of its own Q1.
