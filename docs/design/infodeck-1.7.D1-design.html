InfoDeck — v1.7 · D1 design

Four corrections from 1.7 on your phone. 1) the gap between cards shrinks; 2) Share joins Open and Edit in the expanded row; 3) the “Shared from InfoDeck” footer and its switch are removed entirely; 4) shared files carry their exact names, never the random one the app stores them under. Mock date Thu 10-Sep-2026.

DESIGN ONLY · nothing built · no version bump Reply by tag: “S1 → 4 dp everywhere”, “Q2 → b”, or “defaults” = all recommended
Root causes, checked in the 1.7 source. Item 1: rows carry an 8 dp list gap plus a 7 dp spacer inside sections plus a 1 dp border each — about 16 dp of air between cards. Item 4: FileStore saves every attachment under a UUID.randomUUID() name, and the share URI points at that file, so WhatsApp shows 3f9c2a1e-….pdf instead of Policy document.pdf. Item 3: the footer is wired through a preference; whatever misbehaved, you have asked for it gone, and gone is simpler.

C4 · Exact names

#RuleDetail
1Storage unchangedFiles stay under their UUID on disk — that is what keeps two “Policy document.pdf” apart
2Share copycache/share/<displayName>, written just before the intent; a name clash within one share gets “ (1)” as the zip entries already do
3Name safetyCharacters a filesystem refuses are replaced with “_”; the extension is preserved so the type is still right
4One helperShareFiles.prepare(files) feeds the pill, the follow-up send, mail and the chooser
5CleanupEmptied when the share returns; swept at app start; a failed copy falls back to the stored file with a logged note rather than a failed share
